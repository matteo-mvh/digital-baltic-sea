Satellite

Why the Baltic Sea?

A sea that is enclosed, connected and vulnerable.

Almost enclosed

Water exchange is limited, so changes can linger for years.

Strong gradients

Salinity, temperature and ecology shift dramatically across the region.

Human pressure

Shipping, nutrients, contaminants and fishing all shape the sea.

Climate change

Warming, oxygen depletion and ecosystem change are already visible.
